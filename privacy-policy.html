Privacy Policy

Steps to Kindness mobile app
Last updated: June 2, 2026

Steps to Kindness provides product information to help users make informed choices about Jain, vegan, vegetarian, and cruelty-free products. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users of the Steps to Kindness mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Search terms and app interaction events, such as searches and app opens, through Firebase Analytics.
  • Product request information that you choose to submit, such as product name, product details, contributor or display name, and contact information.
  • Technical data needed to provide the app, such as network request data, device and app diagnostics, and server logs.
  • Camera access, if a barcode scanning feature is enabled and you choose to use it. Camera access is used to scan barcodes and is not used to collect photos for marketing.

Information You Provide Through Forms

The app may open a Google Form or another request form so users can request new product entries. These forms may ask for product details, a contributor or display name, and contact information so requests can be reviewed. Your contact information is used for review or follow-up and is not shown publicly. Contributor names may be used for helper counts or leaderboard features if submitted and approved.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide search results, product details, product images, and leaderboard information.
  • Review and process product requests submitted by users.
  • Understand app usage and improve reliability, performance, and content quality.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, invalid submissions, and inappropriate content.
  • Communicate with users when needed about submitted product requests.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase, used for analytics and app usage measurement.
  • Google Cloud services, used to host app server APIs and store or process product request data.
  • Google Forms, when users choose to submit product request information through a form.
  • External image and web services, used to load product images, app images, or linked web pages.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as analytics, hosting, database, form, or security providers. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to protect the app from fraud, abuse, or security risks.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the app, review product requests, maintain leaderboard records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve our services. Product request records may be retained so that the product database can be reviewed, corrected, and improved over time.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit product request forms or optional contact information. You can also deny camera permission if prompted, although barcode-related features may not work without it. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you submitted, contact us using the details below.

International Users

The app and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own. By using the app, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed in locations where privacy laws may differ from those in your region.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies.

Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact the Steps to Kindness developer using the developer contact listed on the Google Play Store listing.
